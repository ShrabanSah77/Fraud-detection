How AML Screening Works

1. Normalize input and encode categorical attributes.

2. Use the trained model to estimate similarity to the dataset's “Illegal” label.

3. Apply explainable indicators for large value, cross-border, offshore/crypto, repeated activity, and authority reports.

4. Combine scores and route suspicious cases for analyst review.

Structuring, layering, and beneficial ownership cannot be reliably proven with these 10 columns. Additional customer history, counterparty IDs and account networks would be needed.
